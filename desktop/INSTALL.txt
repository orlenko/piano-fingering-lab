Fingering Studio — evaluation build

Requirements
Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 14 or later, and several GB of free space
for the app and projects. This build has been checked on the development Mac;
your first run on another Mac is still a compatibility test.

You do not need Siri, Apple Intelligence, an API key, Python, Xcode or a paid AI
subscription. Analysis runs locally and works offline after installation.
Internet is needed to download the installer and may be used by macOS to verify
the app when first opened. Normal file-access prompts may appear for protected
folders. The app analyzes saved files; it does not use the live camera/microphone.

Install
1. Open the DMG and copy Fingering Studio into Applications.
   Drag the app onto the Applications shortcut, or use Finder's Copy and Paste.
2. Eject the DMG.
3. Open Fingering Studio from Applications.
If macOS cannot verify the app, stop and contact the person who supplied it.
No security setting changes or bypass commands are part of installation.

Try it
Choose or drop one recording and its score PDF. Use a short recording with piano
audio: up to 30 seconds, 1080p, and 1,000 frames. Score support is limited to some
digitally typeset editions; scans and many notation exports are not supported.
Keep both hands and the bass end visible; current calibration expects the camera
behind the music desk and a clear hand-free view during the clip.

Choose a project location and analyze. Select notes to review estimates. Save
corrections, then Update video & score before exporting. Automatic Likely and
Uncertain labels are heuristic estimates; check them against the actual hands.

This is a private evaluation build. Third-party notices remain inside the app.
Public redistribution needs its own clearance; notarization is not a license or
a guarantee of musical accuracy. Your originals and saved projects stay local.
